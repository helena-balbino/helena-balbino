## 📂 Selected Projects

### [Churn Prediction – Telecom](https://github.com/helena-balbino/x_telecom_part2)
Predictive churn model using Random Forest with 73% recall in identifying at-risk telecom customers.
The project applies cross-validation and hyperparameter optimization to ensure robust generalization across different customer profiles. By accurately flagging customers with higher churn propensity, the solution supports proactive retention strategies, enabling data-driven prioritization of customer engagement and loyalty actions.

---

### [Fuel Market Exploration – Brazil](https://github.com/helena-balbino/fuel-market-exploration-brazil)
Large-scale exploratory analysis of fuel prices in Brazil using millions of ANP records from 2019 to 2024. The project consolidates and cleans nationwide fuel price data to analyze temporal trends, regional price dispersion, and differences across fuel types. By revealing patterns of volatility and regional disparities, the analysis supports data-driven insights for economic monitoring, pricing strategies, and decision-making in energy and logistics-related contexts.

---

### [Absenteeism at Work](https://github.com/helena-balbino/absentismo_at_work)
Multiclass absenteeism prediction model improving recall on minority absence levels through SMOTE balancing. Using a Multiclass Logistic Regression applied to the UCI Absenteeism at Work dataset, the project transformed continuous absence hours into actionable absence levels for HR decision-making. After addressing severe class imbalance with SMOTE, the model achieved ~61% overall accuracy and significantly improved recall for minority classes, including 67% recall for the “No Absence” class and stable F1-scores (~0.67–0.68) for moderate and high absence levels. The solution demonstrates how interpretable models can support early identification of absenteeism patterns, workforce planning, and cost reduction strategies in people management.

---

### [Bankruptcy Prediction](https://github.com/helena-balbino/bankruptcy)
Bankruptcy risk prediction model achieving ROC-AUC 0.93 on unseen data, designed as a decision-support tool for financial risk assessment. Using financial indicators from the Taiwanese Bankruptcy Prediction dataset, the project frames bankruptcy prediction as a risk-sensitive classification problem, explicitly addressing severe class imbalance and asymmetric misclassification costs. A Neural Network model with decision-threshold tuning achieved 66.7% recall and 0.48 F1-score on the bankruptcy class, significantly improving minority-class balance compared to a recall-heavy logistic regression baseline. The results demonstrate how machine learning models can support early warning systems, credit analysis, and due diligence processes, enhancing risk prioritization without replacing expert financial judgment.

---

### [Coffee Bean Anomaly Detection using Variational Autoencoder (VAE)](https://github.com/helena-balbino/coffee-bean-anomaly-detection-vae)
Automated visual inspection system for coffee beans using unsupervised deep learning, achieving ROC–AUC 0.91 in defect detection without labeled defect data. Developed a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) trained exclusively on healthy coffee bean images to learn patterns of visual normality and identify defective beans through reconstruction error. The model demonstrated strong discriminative capacity, with 84.7% sensitivity and 81.5% specificity at an optimized decision threshold, effectively separating defective from non-defective samples. This approach addresses real-world quality inspection challenges where defective samples are rare, heterogeneous, or costly to label, supporting scalable, objective, and automated quality control in agribusiness and food production environments.
